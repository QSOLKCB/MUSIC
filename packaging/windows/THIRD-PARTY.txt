MUSIC portable Windows preview

MUSIC is Apache-2.0 licensed; see LICENSE. miniaudio has its own license in
MINIAUDIO-LICENSE.txt. GTK and the other dynamically linked libraries retain
their separate licenses, including LGPL terms. See share/licenses for the
dependency notices and license texts installed by MSYS2.
Some included notices also cover build-only packages.

runtime-packages.txt records the installed versions, upstream URLs and license
identifiers for packages owning bundled DLLs. manifest.json maps those DLLs to
packages and records the hashes of every packaged file. build-packages.txt
records the complete toolchain/package inventory, not just runtime libraries.

MSYS2 package recipes, patches and source information are available at:
https://github.com/msys2/MINGW-packages
https://packages.msys2.org/
https://repo.msys2.org/mingw/sources/

This is an unsigned, locally built engineering preview, not a redistributable
binary release. Do not publish or share the dependency-bearing ZIP until the
matching corresponding-source archives and build recipes have been collected,
made available with the binaries as required, and the redistribution obligations
of every bundled dependency have been reviewed. Calling a package a preview or
using dynamic linkage does not remove those obligations. CI therefore uploads
only validation logs, inventories and hashes, never this ZIP or bundled DLLs.

Do not remove the dependency notices. Dynamic libraries remain replaceable;
MUSIC does not prevent users from substituting compatible library builds.
